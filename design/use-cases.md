### Joining a game
On the menu screen, the player enters their Tribe name, as well as an optional team code that will allow them to be on the same team as their friends. If they don't enter a team code, they will be randomly assigned to one of the two default teams. Once they click the "Play" button, they will be taken to the game screen, where they will be placed in a random location on the map, and their tribe will be spawned with one axe tribesman and one bow tribesman.

### Clearing a meadow
The player drags their totem to move, navigating to the nearest meadow as seen on their minimap. Once in range, their axeman will automatically start chopping down the tree in the meadow, while the bowman will idle, since there is nothing to shoot nearby. The player clicks on the mushroom growing in the meadow, at which point the bowman will eat it, gaining the tribe one food. After the tree is chopped down, it drops two wood resources on the ground, which the tribesmen will pick up.

### Fighting a barbarian
On the outskirts of the map, the player finds a singular barbarian with lvl one armor and a hammer. The hammer does the same amount of damage as the axeman's axe, but the barbarian has one more health (4) than the axeman (3). The tribesmen start attacking eachother, with the axeman attacking the barbarian and the bowman shooting arrows at it. Because of the numbers advantage, the player wins the fight, but the axeman takes two points of damage, and is at 1 health. The player clicks on the campfire icon on the side of the screen, and places it on the ground. The player clicks the campfire again to light it up, and the tribesmen sit down around it, spending food to heal themselves.

### Leveling up
Once the player gets two XP, they level up for the first time, selecting the lvl 1 armor upgrade. They then click on the newly added "lether cap" button on the side of the screen, and click on their axeman to equip it, spending one gold and giving their melee unit one more health.

### Adding a new tribesman
The player decides to purchase a new tribesman, and clicks the tribesman icon on the side of the screen, clicking in empty space on the map to place it. This time it costs 2 food, but this will scale as the tribe's population increases. The player then purchases a bow for three wood, and equips it to the new tribesman.
