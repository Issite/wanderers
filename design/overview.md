Wanderers.io is a multiplayer survival game where players gather resources and fight enemies in a small open world. Instead of controlling a single character, players control a tribe of up to six tribesmen, who each accomplish tasks accoring to their priority list. Players can direct their tribe by moving their tribe's totem, which is the central point of the tribe, by dragging it with the mouse. They can also purchase items like campfires or new tools to equip their tribesmen with, and interact with certain objects in the world, like trade buildings or mushrooms.
The player's main goal is to increase their score, which they can do in multiple ways, but gathering and maintaining resources is always a key priority. Each of the three main resources (food, wood and gold), come from a different source: food is gathered by hunting animals, wood is gathered by chopping trees and gold is gathered by mining rocks. However, these actions can only be performed by tribesmen with the right tools, so players need to make sure they have the right tools and enough resources to keep their tribe alive and growing. In addition, resources can only be found in "meadows", patches of ground that regrow their resources periodically. As one approaches the center of the map, meadows become larger and more fruitful, but also attract more players and larger barbarian tribes.
If two players on different teams meet, their tribes will fight each other, until one of them either dies or runs away. There are also barbarian tribes that sit on top of resources and attack players who get too close. Killing enemy tribesmen is one of the main ways to increase your score, but losing even a single tribesman with expensive equipment can be a huge setback, especially if they were the only one with a particular tool that the player needs to rebuild. To heal any damaged troop, players can use campfires, which cost wood, but allow tribesment to sit and rest, spending food to heal themselves.
As players increase their score, they will level up, allowing them to pick upgrades that give them access to new tools, armor, and abilities. Only a certian number of upgrade opportunities are available in each run, so players need to pick which ones are most useful for their current situation, and which ones are most powerful overall.
